# ⚙️ RAT KING RECON — PATCH NOTES ## v1.0.1 *"A few loose bolts tightened, a few rats put back where they belong."* --- ### 🆕 New Contraptions **⚔️ Battle Rat System** You can now designate a specific rat as your **Battle Rat** — marked with a badge/icon right in the Ledger — so *you* choose who steps into the scrapyard, instead of leaving it to the machine's whims. - Your Battle Rat is locked out of **Expeditions**, **Scrap Run**, and **Ledger Tasks** while on duty — a fighter earning its keep can't also be off running errands. A real trade-off between combat power and passive income. - Reassigning your Battle Rat is **free and instant** — swap freely, experiment, find your champion. - If your Battle Rat gets knocked out or fed into the Splicing Vat, it steps down gracefully on its own — no jammed gears, nothing left locked up. **🛠️ Debug Combat Trigger** *(dev builds only)* An internal tool to force a combat encounter on demand — purely for testing, not something you'll see in the live build. --- ### 🔧 Tinkering & Fixes **📋 Contract Board — Active Contract View** Previously, tapping a bounty while one was already in progress just dead-ended with a "you already have one" popup. Now you'll see your **active contract's progress, time remaining, and reward** right there — no more guessing what you signed up for. **🏷️ Real Package ID** Swapped the placeholder `com.example.ratkingrecon` for the real applicationId — a necessary bolt to tighten ahead of the Play Store submission. **📈 Power Surge / Golden Wrench Scaling** These combat buffs were flat bonuses that quietly lost their punch against tougher bosses. They're now **percentage-based**, so they scale properly no matter how big the Rustbot on the other side of the fight is. **🧬 Fusion Pot Roster Drift** 18 rat species that were previously unreachable through Splicing are now obtainable. The gene pool just got a lot deeper. --- ### 🧹 Workshop Cleanup - Removed dead code left over from earlier builds - Fixed inconsistent key references across systems - Extracted hardcoded strings for cleaner localization down the line --- *Keep walking, keep splicing. The scrapyard's always growing.*
